Privacy Policy

Texting English · Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

Texting English ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its developer to create educational videos and upload them to the developer's own YouTube channel. The app is not distributed to the public and is not used by other people.

Information we access

When the developer signs in with Google, the app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope. This permission is used solely to upload videos (with their titles, descriptions and tags) to the developer's own YouTube channel.

Information we collect and store

The app does not collect personal information from any visitor or user. The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only on the developer's own computer and is used only to perform uploads. No data is sent to, or stored on, any third-party server operated by the app.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose any Google user data to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

The stored OAuth token can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file, or revoked from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: fivepeal@gmail.com
