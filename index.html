Texting English

English-learning YouTube Shorts, made from everyday couple text messages.

About

Texting English is a small personal tool that creates short educational videos (YouTube Shorts) for Korean learners of English. Each video shows a KakaoTalk-style text conversation between a couple and explains the real nuance of the English phrases used.

The app is used only by its developer to upload the videos it creates to the developer's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not ask other people to sign in.

YouTube API usage

The app requests the youtube.upload scope solely to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the developer's own channel. It does not read, analyze or store any other YouTube or Google account data.
